# MiniModel P2P Proof Ladder

This is a prelaunch proof sequence, not a claim that a public P2P lane exists.

1. Validate bounded catalog and composition schemas with positive, negative,
   mutation, property, and fuzz corpora.
2. Prove deterministic route selection with no hidden retry or route switch.
3. Prove fixed piece boundaries, per-piece hashes, identity agreement, bounded
   concurrency, cancellation, durable progress, and corruption quarantine.
4. Prove complete-member and full-composition verification before atomic
   activation.
5. Prove a secure origin-bound loopback companion session with explicit user
   presence and no arbitrary commands, paths, or URLs.
6. Run one real pinned local conversion and preserve its exact private and
   public-safe evidence.
7. Establish a qualified small starter on the authorized MiniModel.org server
   seed and prove its transfer across an independently observed outside-network
   lane. Verify automatic first use and cached startup without local files,
   Hugging Face setup or manual peer/IP configuration.
8. Activate the exact composition in a real browser and complete the required
   numerical, privacy, cancellation, and clean-profile gates.
9. Publish only from a clean pushed revision, then verify exact public HTTPS
   bytes and release records.

Localhost, LAN, mocked, fixture, configured, or reachable states do not satisfy
the outside-network gate. Campaign progress remains unchanged until exact
matching receipts exist.
