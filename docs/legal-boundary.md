# MiniModel Privacy, Consent, And Legal Boundary

MiniModel.org provides technical catalog and discovery metadata. A catalog row
is not legal advice, a license grant, a quality endorsement, or authorization
to redistribute bytes.

## Source And License

Every supported source binds an immutable revision, source-file locks,
publisher identity, model-card route, license route, and review status. Local
conversion stops when source provenance or license authority is ambiguous.

## Peer Consent

A peer announcement is scoped, expiring, composition-bound, and explicit. A
receiver verifies consent and identity before requesting pieces. A sender sees
and approves the lane it serves. Neither party receives hidden route retries or
an unrelated byte request.

## Privacy

Project sites do not receive prompts or model bytes. Public records exclude:

- prompts, transcripts, generated text, and private memory payloads;
- passwords, session tokens, invitation URLs, and account identifiers;
- local filesystem paths and machine-specific details; and
- raw private qualification, conversion, browser, or hosted-memory evidence.

Model bytes move only from an explicitly selected public source to the local
companion or directly between consenting peers.

## Companion Authority

The browser-to-companion protocol is origin-bound, authenticated,
user-approved, short-lived, and restricted to typed model intents. It does not
accept arbitrary commands, arbitrary paths, or unrestricted URLs from a page.

## MemoryEndpoints.com Separation

MemoryEndpoints.com is authorized for direct ecosystem-agent coordination and,
separately, as the user-selected long-term-memory plugin for TinyRustLM.com
Connect. Those lanes have separate identity, credentials, consent, scopes,
provenance, idempotency, and receipts. Neither is MiniModel model-byte
transport.
